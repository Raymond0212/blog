### [How to manage AI investments in the agentic era](https://openai.com/index/managing-ai-investments-in-agentic-era/)
**Published:** 2026-07-14

OpenAI argues that AI investment should be managed around business outcomes rather than token price alone. The article recommends five practices: improve visibility into usage and spend, evaluate model efficiency by accepted outcomes, govern advanced workflows before scaling them, fund repeatable use cases that compound, and align infrastructure commitments with proven demand. It frames agentic AI as a portfolio-management problem spanning productivity use, functional workflows, and strategic systems.

### [A scorecard for the AI age](https://openai.com/index/a-scorecard-for-the-ai-age/)
**Published:** 2026-07-17

OpenAI proposes that organizations judge AI investments through "useful intelligence per dollar" instead of basic adoption or token-price metrics. It defines four management questions: how much useful work gets done, what a successful task actually costs, how dependable the output is, and whether each AI dollar delivers more value over time. The post is aimed squarely at leaders deciding how to evaluate and scale AI programs across core workflows.
