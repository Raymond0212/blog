### [OpenAI frontier models and Codex are now available on AWS](https://openai.com/index/openai-frontier-models-and-codex-are-now-available-on-aws/)
**Published:** 2026-06-01

OpenAI announced that its frontier models and Codex are now generally available through AWS, aimed at enterprises that want to deploy OpenAI capabilities inside existing AWS procurement, billing, security, compliance, and governance workflows. The post frames this as a production-readiness move: OpenAI models are available via Amazon Bedrock, while Codex is positioned as an AWS-native software engineering agent for writing, reviewing, debugging, and modernizing code. It also signals a future AWS path for Daybreak, including cyber models and Codex Security.

### [Codex for every role, tool, and workflow](https://openai.com/index/codex-for-every-role-tool-workflow/)
**Published:** 2026-06-02

OpenAI announced a broader Codex push beyond software engineering, centered on role-specific plugins for teams such as analytics, creative, sales, design, and finance. The release also adds richer annotation workflows so users can refine specific parts of documents, slides, spreadsheets, and sites in place, plus a preview of Sites for sharing interactive internal apps and websites by URL within a workspace. The announcement positions Codex as a cross-functional work platform, with admins retaining control through plugin app approvals and site access settings.

### [Introducing new capabilities to GPT-Rosalind](https://openai.com/index/introducing-new-capabilities-to-gpt-rosalind/)
**Published:** 2026-06-03

OpenAI announced a GPT-Rosalind update for life sciences research that combines GPT-5.5-style agentic coding and tool use with stronger domain performance in medicinal chemistry, genomics, quantitative biology, and wet-lab troubleshooting. The post says the model improves on LifeSciBench, MedChemBench, GeneBench, and LabWorkBench, and pairs the model with Codex-based Life Sciences Research and NGS Analysis plugins plus native scientific file viewers. This makes the announcement notable as a workflow-oriented research product rather than a raw benchmark release.

### [Dreaming: Better memory for a more helpful ChatGPT](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-memory-dreaming/)
**Published:** 2026-06-04

OpenAI announced a new ChatGPT memory architecture built on dreaming, a background synthesis system meant to keep personalization fresher, more relevant, and more scalable than the older saved-memories approach. The update adds a reviewable memory summary so users can see and correct what ChatGPT knows about them, while OpenAI says the rollout began in the United States for Plus and Pro users on June 4, 2026. The supporting materials make clear that this is as much a systems architecture update as a feature launch, with new controls around how remembered context is reviewed and managed.
