### [Our approach to EU text provenance rules](https://openai.com/index/eu-text-provenance/)

**Published:** 2026-10-05

OpenAI introduced opt-in textGrain watermarking for selected API models worldwide and announced a phased rollout to eligible ChatGPT and Codex outputs in the EU in response to AI Act transparency obligations. Its linked technical report describes a statistical watermark that balances detectability with sampling diversity; detector access initially remains restricted to approved researchers and expert organizations. Detection becomes unreliable for short, constrained, or heavily edited passages, and a watermark establishes neither authorship nor factual accuracy, making it a limited provenance signal rather than proof of responsibility.

Supporting reading: [Supporting material 1](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/e9508624-d767-41b6-a26d-e34ca798ada6/textgrain-entropy-calibrated-watermarking-for-language-model-text.pdf); [Supporting material 2](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/code-practice-ai-generated-content); [Supporting material 3](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8912793-provenance-signals-in-openai-generated-content).
