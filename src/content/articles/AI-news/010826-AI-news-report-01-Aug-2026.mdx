## Executive Summary

This week was unusually dense and concentrated around production-scale AI operations rather than consumer feature churn. OpenAI dominated the period with seven posts spanning infrastructure economics, benchmark methodology, scientific workflows, workplace adoption, and academic distribution, while Anthropic published one important protocol update focused on MCP deployability and governance. The clearest pattern is that both vendors are pushing AI further into operational systems: lower-cost model tiers, better harness design, stronger enterprise controls, and more standardized agent infrastructure.

**Key updates:**

- OpenAI positioned GPT-5.6 efficiency gains as a full-stack strategy, pairing lower Luna and Terra pricing with faster Sol processing and internal serving optimizations.
- OpenAI argued that benchmark outcomes depend heavily on harness choices, showing ARC-AGI-3 scores improve sharply when reasoning state and compaction are preserved.
- OpenAI expanded distribution into research institutions with a program for 100,000 academic researchers using ChatGPT, ChatGPT Work, and Codex.
- OpenAI published a field report showing coding agents can materially accelerate scientific software modernization, while moving the bottleneck toward validation and stewardship.
- Anthropic announced support for the MCP `2026-07-28` spec, moving the protocol toward stateless HTTP-style operation with stronger OAuth/OIDC-ready authorization patterns.

**Key trends:**

- AI vendors are increasingly selling outcome economics, workflow throughput, and infrastructure efficiency rather than raw model novelty alone.
- Agent performance is being framed as a systems problem where harness design, context handling, and tooling choices materially affect results.
- Enterprise adoption is maturing around governance and standardization, with more emphasis on privacy-controlled workspaces, interoperable protocols, and production-ready authorization.

The article is a strong reminder that agent benchmarks are deeply sensitive to API and harness design choices.
